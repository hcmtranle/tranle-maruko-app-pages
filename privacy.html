Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Tranle Maruko Auto Post is a personal, single-user automation tool. It does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any third party.

The only data this application accesses is the TikTok account access token for @tranle.maruko, granted directly by the account owner through TikTok's official OAuth authorization flow, and used exclusively to upload video content created by the owner.

No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than publishing the owner's own video content to their own TikTok account.
